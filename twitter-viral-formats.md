# X/Twitter Viral Post Formats

> Research-backed. Algorithm-optimized. Plug-and-play.

---

## Algorithm Context (2025–2026)

These formats are designed around the actual weights in the X algorithm:

| Signal | Weight vs a Like |
|---|---|
| Replies | 13.5x – 27x |
| Profile click + engagement | 24x |
| 2+ minutes dwell time | 22x |
| Native images/video | Boosted over text-only |

**Key principle:** Your goal is not likes. Your goal is replies and dwell time. Every format below is designed with this in mind.

**Viral trajectory window:** First 30–60 minutes of engagement determines reach. Post when your audience is active. Reply to comments within 30 minutes (75x boost for reply-to-reply).

## PART 1: SINGLE TWEET FORMATS

### 1. The Contrarian Take
```
[Common belief] is wrong.
[Contrarian position backed by experience/data].
Here's why:
```
**Why it works:** Creates tension that demands resolution. Attracts supporters AND critics — both engage. Positions you as a thought leader who thinks differently.

---

### 2. The Earned Secret
```
I [specific impressive result with numbers].
Here's the [exact/specific] [system/playbook/framework] I used:
```
**Why it works:** Specific numbers create credibility. "Here's" signals actionable value is coming. Opens a curiosity loop that must be closed.

---

### 3. The Story Hook
```
[Time period] ago, I was [relatable struggle].
Today, [impressive transformation].
Here's [what changed/what I learned]:
```
**Why it works:** Humans are wired for narrative transformation. Creates emotional investment before the value drop.

---

### 4. The Bold Statement
```
Nobody talks about this, but [surprising truth].
```
OR
```
Unpopular opinion: [strong position].
```
**Why it works:** Pattern interrupt stops the scroll. Works best with positions you genuinely hold and can defend.

---

### 5. The Question Hook
```
Want to know the real secret to [desirable outcome]?
It's not [common assumption]. It's [surprising truth].
```
**Why it works:** Questions activate the brain's answer-seeking mode. "Real secret" implies insider knowledge.

---

### 6. The Pattern Interrupt
```
Everyone says [common advice].
They're wrong.
Here's what actually works:
```
**Why it works:** Short punchy lines create rhythm. "They're wrong" demands attention. Sets up authority positioning.

---

### 7. The Specific Value Promise
```
Here are [number] [specific things] that [specific benefit]:
```
**Why it works:** Specificity signals quality. Clear benefit tells reader exactly what they'll get.

---

### 8. The "This or That" Engagement Bait
```
[Option A] or [Option B]?
Reply with your pick.
```
**Why it works:** Extremely low barrier to engagement. Replies carry 13.5x the algorithmic weight of likes. Creates debates that boost visibility.

---

### 9. The Fill-in-the-Blank
```
The best advice I ever received was ____.
```
OR
```
The most underrated skill for [audience] is ____.
```
**Why it works:** Makes replying easy and fun. Generates dozens of replies that boost algorithmic distribution.

---

### 10. The Incomplete List
```
Here are [number] ways to [benefit]:
[List items]
What would you add?
```
**Why it works:** Invites audience to complete the list. Generates high reply counts. Creates collaborative engagement.

## PART 2: THREAD FORMATS

Threads are the highest-value content format on X. They maximize dwell time, showcase expertise, and generate the most followers per impression. Use 1–2x per week.

### 11. The 7-Part Viral Thread Structure

| Tweet | Role | What to include |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | Most compelling insight or result. Specific numbers. Curiosity gap. Thread indicator. |
| 2 | Context | Background and credibility. Why should readers trust you? |
| 3–6 | Core Content | ONE key insight per tweet with supporting detail. End each with a cliffhanger. |
| 7 | Summary + CTA | Recap key points. Clear call-to-action (follow, bookmark, which tip resonated). |

---

### 12. The Case Study Breakdown
```
[Company/Person] [achieved remarkable result].
Here's the [strategy/playbook] they used (and how you can copy it):
```
**Why it works:** Borrows authority from known brands/people. "How you can copy it" makes it immediately applicable.

---

### 13. The Curated Resource Thread
```
I spent [X hours/days] [researching/collecting/testing] so you don't have to.
Here are the [number] best [resources] for [specific outcome]:
```
**Why it works:** Shows effort and curation. Extremely high save/bookmark rate.

---

### 14. The "X Things I Wish I Knew" Thread
```
[Number] things I wish I knew before [milestone/experience]:
```
**Why it works:** Positions you as experienced. Appeals to people earlier in the journey. Nostalgia and reflection drive engagement.

---

### 15. The Named Framework Thread
```
I use the [ACRONYM/NAME] Framework for [outcome].
It's helped me [specific result].
Here's how it works:
```
**Why it works:** Named frameworks create recall and shareability. Positions you as the creator of intellectual property.

## PART 3: HIGH-ENGAGEMENT FORMATS

Designed to maximize reply counts (13.5–27x algorithmic weight of likes). Use 2–3x per week.

### 16. Strategic Polls

Best poll types:
- "This or That" debates (low cognitive load, high participation)
- Industry predictions ("In 5 years, which will matter more?")
- Controversial takes ("Which is overrated?")
- Tournament brackets (multiple polls, elimination style)

Always add: "Explain your vote in the comments"

---

### 17. The Hot Take
```
Hot take: [Strong opinion you can defend].
```
Rules: Must be genuinely contrarian. You need to be able to defend it in replies. Controversial but not offensive. Best when backed by experience or data.

---

### 18. The Relatable Observation
```
When you [universal experience]...
```
OR
```
Why does nobody talk about [common but unspoken thing]?
```
**Why it works:** "I relate to this" is the #1 driver of shares. Makes readers feel seen and understood.

---

### 19. The Advice Request
```
What's your experience with [topic]?
I'm [context for why you're asking].
```
**Why it works:** People love sharing their expertise. Creates community and reciprocity.

## PART 4: MEDIA-ENHANCED FORMATS

Native media gets algorithmic preference. Combine with proven hooks for maximum reach.

### 20. The Screenshot Proof Post
```
[Bold claim about result].
[Screenshot as proof]
Here's exactly how:
```
Screenshots are 78% more likely to be retweeted than claims without proof.

---

### 21. The Data Visualization Post
```
[Attention-grabbing data point or insight].
[Chart/infographic showing the data]
```
Best practices: Simple charts beat complex ones. Use 1200x675px (16:9) for optimal display. Bright visuals with consistent brand colors.

---

### 22. The Carousel Post
```
[Hook with value promise]
[4 images: slide 1 = hook, slides 2-3 = content, slide 4 = CTA]
```
Specs: 800x800px (1:1) or 800x418px (1.91:1). Carousels increase engagement by up to 25% vs single images.

---

### 23. The Native Video Post
```
[Text hook that promises the video payoff]
[30–90 second native video with captions]
```
Rules: Under 30 seconds works best. ALWAYS add captions. Native video gets 40% more engagement than linked video. Use 1280x720 (16:9) or 1080x1080 (1:1).

### Critical Success Factors

- Post when your audience is active — first 30–60 mins determine reach
- Reply to comments within 30 minutes (75x boost for reply-to-reply)
- Specific numbers beat vague claims ("12,487 followers" not "lots of followers")
- Keep tweets under 250 characters when possible. Under 200 is even better
- Threads should be 4–8 tweets with visuals every 3–4 tweets
- Pin your best performing tweet to your profile (200% more profile visits)
- Repost successful content 2–4 weeks later to new audiences
- Avoid links in main tweets (algorithm dislikes them). Put links in replies
- Use 1–2 relevant hashtags maximum. More triggers spam filters
- Consistency > frequency. 2–3 high-quality posts/day beats 10 mediocre ones

## Prompt Instructions for LLMs

When using this skill to generate X/Twitter content:

1. **Identify the goal** — growth, engagement, authority, or conversion
2. **Select the format** — match format to goal using the guide above
3. **Apply algorithm logic** — prioritize reply-bait over like-bait
4. **Use specific numbers** — never vague claims
5. **Check hook strength** — would you stop scrolling for this?
6. **Keep it short** — aim for under 200 characters for single tweets
7. **End with a reply prompt** — every post should invite a response
