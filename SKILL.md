## Platform-Specific Strategies

### YouTube (Primary Platform)
- **Thumbnail first** — the thumbnail is designed before the video is filmed. It's the product spec.
- **Title = curiosity gap + stakes** — e.g. "I Spent 50 Hours Buried Alive"
- **First 30 seconds = full video pitch** — viewer decides to stay or leave in the opening hook. Open with the most extreme moment or the highest stakes reveal.
- **Pacing over production** — cuts every 2–4 seconds to maintain energy. Silence is never allowed to settle.
- **Escalation arc** — every video escalates. Stakes go up, participants drop out, tension builds toward the finale.
- **Re-watch value** — embed hidden details, callbacks, or bonus reveals that reward repeat viewing.
- **End screen CTA** — always directs to the next video or subscription.

### YouTube Shorts
- Hook in frame 1 — no intro, no title card, action immediately
- Mirror the long-form video but distill to one moment
- Used for discovery, not depth

### Instagram
- Behind-the-scenes of production or prep
- Stills from the most visually extreme moment of the video
- Caption = short version of the video hook

### X (Twitter)
- Announce videos with the thumbnail + title formula
- Post the "I can't believe this happened" moment as a clip
- Use the Earned Secret format: "We spent $X to film this. Here's what nobody saw:"

### TikTok
- Remix the most shareable 30-second moment
- Duet or stitch challenges to extend reach
- "Would you do this for $X?" drives massive reply engagement

## Hook Formulas

- **Stakes opener:** "The last person to [extreme action] wins $[amount]"
- **Scale hook:** "I [did something] for [impossibly long time / large number]"
- **Access hook:** "Nobody has ever done this before"
- **Payoff tease:** "One of these people is about to become a millionaire"
- **Empathy hook:** "I gave a homeless man $100,000..."

## Prompt Instructions

When channeling MrBeast's content strategy:

1. **Start with the thumbnail concept** — what's the one image and 5-word title that makes someone click?
2. **Design the stakes first** — how high can they go? What's the payoff?
3. **Build an escalation arc** — the content should get more intense as it progresses
4. **Write the hook as if it's the first 10 seconds** — no warmup, instant action
5. **Use simple, universal language** — anyone aged 10–40 should understand immediately
6. **End with a CTA that feels earned** — don't beg for likes, make the action feel natural
7. **Apply to X:** Use "Earned Secret" or "Story Hook" formats from twitter-viral-formats.md with the stakes-first approach
