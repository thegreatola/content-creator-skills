## Creator Overview

Steven Bartlett built his brand on radical vulnerability and the willingness to share things most entrepreneurs hide — failure, mental health struggles, doubt, and the unglamorous reality behind success. He pairs this with world-class interview skills and a genuine curiosity that makes even familiar topics feel new. His content works because it makes high-achievers feel understood and makes aspiring entrepreneurs feel like success is possible.

## Platform-Specific Strategies

### YouTube / Podcast (Primary Platform)
- **Interview architecture** — opens with a statement designed to create instant intrigue ("Before we start, I need to tell you something...")
- **Vulnerability as authority** — sharing his own failures and fears before asking guests about theirs creates psychological safety and depth
- **The long-form bet** — 2–3 hour episodes that create deep parasocial bonds. Listeners feel like they know Steven personally.
- **Episode titles use tension** — "The Man Who Quit His $1M Job to Start Over at 40" not "Entrepreneurship Interview"
- **Clip strategy** — every episode produces 10–20 short clips for other platforms, each with its own hook
- **Guest selection signals values** — guests are chosen to teach specific lessons, not just for fame

### LinkedIn
- **Authority thought leadership** — long posts on business lessons, leadership failures, and mindset
- **Vulnerable confessions** — "Here's what I got wrong about building a business at 21"
- **Numbered insight lists** — "7 things I learned from interviewing 500 CEOs"
- LinkedIn is his second-highest converting platform for personal brand

### X (Twitter)
- **Thread format dominates** — uses "X Things I Wish I Knew" and "Earned Secret" formats heavily
- **Aphorism posts** — short, quotable one-liners from episode conversations
- **Controversial business takes** — "Unpopular opinion: Most startup advice is designed for investors, not founders"
- Cross-posts episode clips as native video

### Instagram
- Designed quotes from episode content (high-save format)
- Behind-the-scenes of studio, guests, and travel
- Reels from the most emotionally resonant episode moments

### TikTok
- "Nobody talks about this in business..." hooks perform best
- Vulnerability moments from long-form that hit universally
- Quick contrarian business takes

## Prompt Instructions

When channeling Steven Bartlett's content strategy:

1. **Lead with vulnerability or a counterintuitive truth** — not a credential or a boast
2. **Write as if you're having a private conversation** — intimate, direct, no performance
3. **Use contrast** — the gap between how things look and how they feel is his most powerful tool
4. **Layer insight with story** — never just give advice, always ground it in experience
5. **Design for saves and shares** — what would someone screenshot and send to a friend?
6. **Apply to X:** Use "Contrarian Take", "Earned Secret", and "X Things I Wish I Knew" formats from twitter-viral-formats.md. Keep language measured and precise — no hype, just truth.
